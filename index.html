Marma Chikitsa

Vital Point Therapy

Marma Chikitsa is an ancient Ayurvedic healing art focused on stimulating 107 vital energy points across the body. By applying precise pressure, massage, or medicated herbal oils, it unblocks the flow of Prana (life energy), relieving pain, improving circulation, and activating the body's natural healing mechanisms.

How It Works

Marma points are intersections where muscles, ligaments, joints, veins, and bones meet. In Ayurveda, these are considered physical doorways to the mind and body. When these points become blocked or disturbed due to injury, stress, or poor posture, it disrupts the flow of energy and causes pain, stiffness, or disease. The therapy gently releases these blockages, restoring overall balance.

Key Benefits:

Pain management (joints, cervical, shoulder)
Nervous system support
Migraine & headache relief
Anxiety & stress reduction
Detoxification boost
Deep relaxation

During a Session: A trained therapist gently presses or manipulates the affected Marma points, often incorporating medicated Ayurvedic oils to soothe the skin and enhance energy flow.

Panchakarma

Five-Action Detoxification

Panchakarma is a traditional Ayurvedic detoxification and purification therapy designed to balance the body's three doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha). It systematically removes deep-seated toxins (Ama) from tissues using a personalized sequence of oil massages, steam baths, and five primary cleansing actions.

The 5 Core Therapies

  • Vamana: Therapeutic vomiting to clear mucus and toxins
  • Virechana: Purgation therapy to cleanse liver and intestines
  • Basti: Medicated enemas for colon and nerve health
  • Nasya: Nasal therapy for sinuses and mental clarity
  • Raktamokshana: Controlled bloodletting for blood purification

The 3-Step Process:

1

Purva Karma (Preparation)

Internal oleation, Abhyanga massage, steam baths to loosen toxins

2

Pradhana Karma (Cleansing)

Active execution of the five core actions to expel toxins

3

Paschat Karma (Rejuvenation)

Post-cleansing diet and herbal tonics to rebuild Agni

Duration: Typically 7-21 days under strict supervision
Customized: Each program is tailored to your unique Prakriti and Vikriti
